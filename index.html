How it works

  1. Connect your TikTok account

    You sign in with TikTok and approve access. We never see your TikTok password.

  2. Add a video and a time

    Upload the video, write the caption, choose the platforms and pick the publishing time.

  3. It publishes on schedule

    At the chosen time, the video is sent to TikTok through the official Content Posting API and appears on your profile.

What we access on TikTok

What we use

  • user.info.basic: your display name and avatar, so you can see which account is connected.
  • video.publish: to publish the videos you scheduled, with the caption and privacy setting you chose.

What we never do

  • Read your messages, followers or other videos.
  • Post anything you did not schedule yourself.
  • Sell or share your data with advertisers.

You can remove access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. Scheduled posts stop as soon as access is removed.
