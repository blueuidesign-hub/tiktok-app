Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what data n8n-autopost collects when you connect a TikTok account or other social media accounts, how that data is used, and how you can have it deleted.

Data we collect

  • TikTok account data: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar, received through TikTok Login Kit with the user.info.basic scope.
  • Access tokens: the access token and refresh token TikTok issues after you approve access. We use them only to publish the posts you schedule.
  • Content you provide: the videos, photos and captions you schedule, and the publishing time you choose.
  • Publishing results: the post ID and status TikTok returns, so we can show whether a post was published.

How we use the data

We use the data only to publish the content you scheduled, at the time you chose, to the accounts you connected. We do not use it for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Where the data is stored

Tokens and scheduling records are stored in private, access-controlled storage used only to run the service. Media files are stored in private cloud storage until they are published. Photos may be placed temporarily on a media delivery host so that TikTok can fetch them, and are removed after publishing.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. Your content is sent only to the platforms you selected for publishing, through their official APIs. We share data with service providers only as far as needed to store files and run the scheduling system.

Retention

  • Access tokens are kept until you remove access or ask us to delete them.
  • Media files are deleted within 30 days after they are published.
  • Scheduling records are kept for up to 12 months, then deleted.

Removing access and deleting your data

You can remove access at any time in the TikTok app: Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions. To have all your stored data deleted, email EMAIL_CUA_BAN with the subject "Delete my data". We complete deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes are announced by email to connected users.
